## 0.2.0 (2026-10-07)

Second iteration (branch `v2`): the factory on pi-subagents. Repo-defined fresh-context agents, generated briefs with ids checked at the gate, deterministic integration (`integrate.sh`), workflows generated by `workflow.sh`, mattpocock/skills v1.3.1 vendored and pinned by checksum, model families linted, GitLab CI gates G1–G4.

## 0.1.0

First iteration (branch `main`): a personal Pi setup. Agents (architect, builder, reviewer, scout, …), prefixed prompts for plan, parallel, drain and wrap flows, a repo template and a bots kit.
